Hey, welcome to Zoutty! 👋

Turn your recap videos and practice recordings into structured AI lessons. Here's what Zoutty does before you install.

Upload recap videos
Import videos recorded at your lessons, workshops, or festivals. Full videos are preserved in your session gallery.
Speech transcription
Zoutty automatically transcribes spoken explanations, teacher advice, and voice notes into text.
AI lesson breakdown
Tap the wand to turn spoken transcripts into structured technical corrections, drills, and homework.
Organize by teacher & style
Group your lessons into folders by instructor, dance style, or event—securely backed up to the cloud.
💡
How video processing works: Zoutty processes the spoken audio transcription (teacher explanations, cues, and feedback), not the actual video footage. Your original video is saved in your gallery so you can always watch it.
How it works
1
Import a recap video or record a live voice note from your lesson or workshop.
2
Zoutty extracts the audio and creates an accurate speech transcription.
3
Tap the wand to generate structured corrections, drills, and homework from the transcript.
